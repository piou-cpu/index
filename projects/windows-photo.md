Windows is an ongoing photographic practice documenting the window forms of New York City's architectural facades. The window itself. Its shape, repetition, geometry. There is an absurdity to it: going out with a camera, flattening, curating, cataloguing. A body of work that became the foundation for a thesis, a brand, and several years of practice that followed.
