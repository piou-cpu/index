"Thirty Five mm Film and Minolta’s SRT-101 in the Lower East Side” is an editorial publication designed by combining a series of Wikipedia articles with my photography. The result was a story, or description, of: film photography, my camera, and Manhattan’s Lower East Side. 

<figure>
  <img src="images/wikibook/covers.jpg">
  <figcaption>1. Covers and TOC </figcaption>
</figure>

My TOC, made up by a combination of dots and breaks (loosely related to the amount of content in each section), is repeated on each chapter’s title page. The cover plays with the concept of hiding and revealing information, similar to what I was doing in my documentation of this particular neighborhood.
