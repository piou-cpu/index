In the summer of 2025, following a rebrand from Studio Augie to AU Studio, we commissioned designer Sebastian Hunger-Bühler to develop new branding and a subsequent website redesign. Pulling inspiration from various sources, we landed on the concept of zoetropes with hand-drawn illustration. The brand assets consisted of logos, wordmarks, and animated gifs.

<figure>
  <img src="images/aus-rebrand/image1.jpg">
  <figcaption>1.</figcaption>
</figure>

We then got to work re-engineering the studio website, with a focus on displaying the wide range of styles and projects the studio executed upon. Following a series of intimate discussions, we landed on a splash page plus index format, allowing us to show work in both an overview and detailed perspective. Rather than present video in its heavy and unoptimized format, we chose to display the work through a series of gifs; breathing life into the site.

<figure>
  <img src="images/aus-rebrand/image2.jpg">

What followed was an ever-evolving portfolio, gallery, and index. A site that allowed for growth without the pitfalls of confusion.
